How I lead

I've been an engineer, a tester, a researcher and a manager. These are the things I keep coming back to.

01 Start from the person using it

An AI system is only useful if the analyst, the engineer or the customer trusts it enough to act on it. I spend time with users early, and I measure success by whether their day got easier.

02 Evaluation before ambition

If we can't measure it, we can't ship it. Precision, recall, latency and cost are agreed before the first prompt is written, and they stay on the dashboard after launch.

03 Responsible by design

In banking and other regulated industries, explainability, audit trails and human oversight aren't extras. I build them into the architecture so the team never has to bolt them on later.

04 Develop people, not dependencies

A good team should run well when I'm not in the room. I invest in everyone on the team, from junior engineers to team leads, through regular 1:1s, real ownership, honest feedback and clear paths to grow, while keeping standards high through code review and CI/CD.
